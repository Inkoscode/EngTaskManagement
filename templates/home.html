What the portal does

Built for engineering teams that need clear ownership and a record of every change.

Task tracking

Set priority, status, planned start and finish, due date and the system each task relates to.

Clear ownership

Every task belongs to one section and can be assigned to a member of that section.

Full history

Each status or date change is recorded with who made it and when.

Who can do what

Everyone can see all teams' tasks. What you can change depends on your role.

RoleAccess
Overall boss
Sees every team's tasks, and edits and assigns tasks in any section.
Team boss
Edits and reassigns any task in their own section.
Engineer
Creates tasks in their section and assigns them to teammates, edits the tasks they created, updates the status of tasks assigned to them, and adds comments and private notes.
